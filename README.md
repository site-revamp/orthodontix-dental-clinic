# Orthodontix Dental Clinic – Homepage Redesign

## 1. Website Overview

Orthodontix Dental Clinic is a private dental practice located in Deira, Dubai, offering specialist orthodontics, cosmetic dentistry, restorative treatments and general dental care.

The redesigned homepage targets adults, families and patients seeking braces, Invisalign, smile makeovers and comprehensive dental treatments. The design direction focuses on specialist credibility, simplified treatment discovery and a clearer appointment enquiry journey, highlighting Dr. Nazeer Ahmed's experience as a specialist orthodontist.

## 2. Main Improvements

### 1. Simplified Navigation and Treatment Organisation
- Streamlined the presentation of treatment information.
- Organised services into clear categories covering orthodontics, cosmetic dentistry and general dental care.
- Improved access to individual treatment information.

**Problem addressed:** The previous website contained numerous treatment links, promotional messages and information sections, making navigation and treatment discovery difficult.

### 2. Stronger Visual Hierarchy and Appointment Journey
- Introduced a more prominent homepage hero section.
- Increased the visibility of Dr. Nazeer Ahmed and his specialist orthodontic expertise.
- Strengthened appointment booking, telephone and WhatsApp calls to action throughout the homepage.

**Problem addressed:** The previous homepage lacked a clear visual journey connecting the clinic's expertise and services with appointment enquiries.

### 3. Better Treatment and Pricing Information Structure
- Improved the organisation of treatment and pricing information.
- Created clearer sections for presenting available treatments and relevant cost information.
- Added enquiry pathways for patients seeking current treatment prices.

**Problem addressed:** Promotional messages, treatment pricing and consultation information were distributed across different areas, making details harder to understand and compare.

## 3. Design Improvements

- **Visual Presentation:** Established a more professional and polished dental healthcare appearance.
- **Layout:** Improved content organisation and section hierarchy to distinguish treatments, specialist information and contact options.
- **Typography:** Strengthened heading and supporting text hierarchy for easier reading.
- **User Experience:** Simplified navigation and made appointment and WhatsApp actions easier to identify.
- **Consistency:** Created a more structured presentation of treatment categories, promotional information and enquiry options.
- **Overall Appearance:** Positioned specialist orthodontic expertise more prominently within the clinic's digital experience.

## Project Objective

The redesigned homepage aims to strengthen Orthodontix Dental Clinic's specialist positioning, make dental treatments easier to explore and provide a clearer path from discovering services to requesting an appointment.
